Covered Outdoor Living

NorfolkDavid Streek · Design & build

A room without walls.

A generous timber canopy extends the living space beyond the house, creating a sheltered place to gather, dine and relax. Warm timber overhead, natural stone underfoot and planting at the edges make the transition between home and garden feel effortless.
