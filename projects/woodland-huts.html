Selected work / Timber structures

Woodland Huts

Ringland, NorfolkDavid Streek · Design & build

Quietly at home among the trees.

A pair of compact, dark-clad timber huts set within the woodland at The Keeper and the Dell. Simple forms, considered proportions and restrained detailing let the buildings sit comfortably among the trees, while the interiors offer warmth and character.
