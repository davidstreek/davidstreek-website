Woodland huts

Ringland, NorfolkSelf-initiated timber buildings

Two huts among the trees.

Two compact, dark-clad timber huts built in the woodland at The Keeper and the Dell in Ringland. The buildings sit among the trees, with straightforward forms and warm interiors.

The huts were built for our own site at The Keeper and the Dell.
