Built from the ground up.

Designed and built from scratch, Keeper’s Cabin is a characterful woodland retreat at The Keeper and the Dell. Its generous covered veranda and raised deck connect the cabin to the landscape, while exposed timber, a wood-burning stove and richly coloured interiors make it a place to stay a while.
