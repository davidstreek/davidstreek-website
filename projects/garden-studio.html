Garden Studio

Garden studio · Permitted development

A studio shaped by its site.

A bespoke garden studio built on a tight site with difficult access and substantial changes of level. Two connected volumes, with slightly different rooflines, are finished in dark vertical timber cladding and generous glazing.

The building includes bathroom and utility facilities, with fitted joinery inside. The project also involved groundworks, a new patio and landscaping around the studio.

The studio was built under permitted development, rather than through the full planning permission used for City Kitchen & Studio.
