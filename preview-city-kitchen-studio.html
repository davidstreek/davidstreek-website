City kitchen & studio

Kitchen extension & separate garden studio · Full planning

Two buildings, one garden.

This project combines a kitchen extension and a separate garden studio, both of which required full planning permission. The kitchen extension was designed by Mole Architects; I designed the garden studio.

The kitchen opens through wide glazed doors to a covered deck and garden. Exposed timber beams and roof glazing run through the extension. Across the garden, the separate studio has full-height glazing and its own timber deck.
