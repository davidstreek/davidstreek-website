Visitor privacy

We count which projects people reach, which photographs they open and how long the images stay visible. These totals help us choose photographs and improve the portfolio. We also count enquiry actions, broad location, screen size and how visits reach the site.

Cloudflare hosts the website and stores daily totals for us. The report contains no names, contact details, IP addresses, form text or individual browsing histories. Contact details you send through the enquiry form go to our enquiry inbox so we can reply.

You can exclude this browser at any time. A preference cookie remembers your choice. We also honour your browser’s Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control setting.
