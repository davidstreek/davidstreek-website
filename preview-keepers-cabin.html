Country cabin

Ringland, NorfolkSelf-initiated cabin build

Keeper’s Cabin.

Keeper’s Cabin is a woodland retreat that I built at The Keeper and the Dell in Ringland. A covered veranda and raised deck connect the building with its surroundings; inside, exposed timber and a wood-burning stove give the cabin its character.

The cabin is part of our own site at The Keeper and the Dell.
