I'm David Streek. For more than 30 years, I've been designing and crafting outdoor spaces that feel like a natural extension of the home.

Working from my own workshop and design studio, I bring together creative thinking, traditional craftsmanship and contemporary materials. Every project begins with the place, and the people who use it.
