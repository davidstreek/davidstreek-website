Covered terrace · Norfolk

Designer garden studio

Bespoke garden studio

Two connected volumes clad in dark vertical timber make up this garden studio, built on a tight site with difficult access and substantial changes of level. The project included a bathroom, utility facilities, fitted joinery, a new patio and landscaping. The studio was built under permitted development.

03 / 06

Country cabin

Cabin · Ringland

I built Keeper’s Cabin as a woodland retreat at The Keeper and the Dell in Ringland. A covered veranda and raised deck open onto the woodland, while exposed timber and a wood-burning stove give the interior its character.

04 / 06

Woodland huts

Timber huts · Ringland

Two compact, dark-clad timber huts sit among the trees at The Keeper and the Dell in Ringland. I built them for our own woodland site.

05 / 06

City kitchen extension with garden studio

Kitchen extension & garden studio

The kitchen extension, designed by Mole Architects, opens through wide glazed doors onto a covered deck and garden. I designed the separate garden studio, which has full-height glazing and its own timber deck. Both buildings required full planning permission.

Countryside studio

Countryside building

Roof glazing along one side of the pitched roof brings daylight into the vaulted studio and across the exposed timber beams. A wide opening leads directly out to the gravel courtyard.

About

An eye for clean lines within natural settings provides the starting point for a sketch.

The design then takes shape through an understanding of materials, engineering and the practicalities of construction. Each decision informs the next, from the first lines on paper to the finished space.

I work with a small team of labourers and bring in specialists as the job needs them.
