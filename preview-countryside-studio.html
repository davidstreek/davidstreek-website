Countryside studio

Countryside studio

Light from above.

Inside this countryside studio, a vaulted ceiling leaves the timber roof beams exposed. Roof glazing runs along one side of the pitched ceiling, bringing daylight into the room.

A wide opening leads directly outside. The space is open beneath the roof, with daylight coming in from the side windows and the roof lights.
